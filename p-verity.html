Every night, the right three.

Verity is an AI chief of staff. Each evening it reads every project, the next day's calendar, and the inbox, then picks the three things that matter most tomorrow. Everything else stays visible, but off the critical path.

It leads with whatever is waiting on you, names anything that has gone stale, and keeps the whole picture in one source of truth. It is used every day inside LionRose and is still in testing.
