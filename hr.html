Compliance

California employment law is complex - and the cost of getting it wrong is high. We help companies navigate worker classification (W-2 vs. 1099), wage and hour requirements, required workplace documentation, leave management, and policy development. We also build employee handbooks and HR policy frameworks that are legally sound, readable, and actually followed. Whether you're a startup building your first HR infrastructure or an established company cleaning up legacy gaps, we bring the rigor the situation requires.

  • Worker Classification
  • Wage & Hour
  • Employee Handbooks
  • Policy Development
  • Leave Management
  • I-9 Compliance
