// Overview
KnowEx · In Development

Learning that sticks.

KnowEx turns books and audiobooks into a personal study library. Each one is broken into chapters, key points, flashcards, and quizzes, so nothing you read is lost when you close the cover.

Then it teaches. Voice quizzes, spoken drills for the drive, and recall check-ins after you listen, all weighted toward what you still need to learn. It is in daily use today for professional exam prep.

// What It Does

Book capture

Voice quizzes

Drills for the drive

Flashcards turned into spoken audio drills.

Recall after listening

Say what you remember and it builds the review.
